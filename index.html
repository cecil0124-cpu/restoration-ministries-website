A Place to Belong

Restoration Ministries is a faith community built on the belief that every person deserves hope, healing, and a place to call home. Whether you're taking your first step of faith or looking to deepen your walk with God, you are welcome here.

We gather together to worship, serve our neighbors, and grow in relationship with one another and with God. No matter your background or story, there is a seat at the table for you.
