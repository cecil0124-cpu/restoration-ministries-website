Programs & Services

Ministries for every season of life — come grow with us.

Worship Services

Our Sunday and midweek gatherings are the heartbeat of our community.

🙏

Sunday Morning Worship

Spirit-filled worship, inspiring messages from God's Word, and a welcoming atmosphere for the entire family. We offer two identical services so you can choose the time that works best for you.

📅 Every Sunday · 9:00 AM & 11:00 AM
📖

Wednesday Bible Study

A mid-week gathering focused on deeper study of Scripture, prayer, and fellowship. All are welcome, whether you're new to the Bible or have been studying for years.

📅 Every Wednesday · 7:00 PM
🌅

Prayer & Intercession

A dedicated time of corporate prayer and intercession for our congregation, community, and nation. Come with your burdens and leave with peace.

📅 Every Friday · 6:00 AM

Family Ministries

Ministries designed to help every member of your family grow in faith.

👶

Children's Ministry (Ages 0–11)

A safe, fun, and faith-filled environment where children learn about God's love through age-appropriate lessons, crafts, worship, and games. We believe children are never too young to encounter Jesus.

📅 During All Sunday Services
🎒

Youth Ministry (Ages 12–18)

A dynamic ministry for middle and high school students — a place where teens can ask real questions, build genuine friendships, and discover who they are in Christ. Weekly services, special events, and retreats throughout the year.

📅 Sundays 11:00 AM & Fridays 7:00 PM
💑

Marriage & Family Enrichment

Classes, workshops, and counseling designed to strengthen marriages and build healthy family relationships. We host regular marriage enrichment evenings and family retreats.

📅 Monthly Workshops · Counseling by Appointment

Community Outreach

Serving our neighbors — because faith without works is incomplete.

🍞

Community Food Pantry

Every week, we provide fresh and non-perishable food to families in need. Volunteers are always welcome to help pack and distribute. No appointment needed for recipients.

📅 Saturdays · 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM
💬

Counseling & Support Services

Our licensed counselors and trained ministry team offer confidential counseling for individuals, couples, and families dealing with grief, addiction, trauma, and life transitions. All services are provided with compassion and without judgment.

📅 Tuesday – Thursday · By Appointment
🎓

Adult Education & Literacy

We partner with local organizations to offer free adult literacy classes, GED preparation, and financial literacy workshops to empower members of our community.

📅 Tuesdays & Thursdays · 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Worship & Arts

Expressing our faith through music, creative arts, and media.

🎵

Praise & Worship Team

Our talented worship team leads the congregation in powerful, Spirit-led worship each Sunday. If you have a musical gift and a heart for God, we'd love to hear from you.

📅 Rehearsals: Thursdays 7:00 PM
🎭

Drama & Creative Arts Ministry

Using the arts to tell the greatest story ever told. Our drama team performs during special services, holiday productions, and community events throughout the year.

📅 Rehearsals: Saturdays 10:00 AM
