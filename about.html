About Us

Our story, our mission, and the people who make it happen.

Our Story

Restoration Ministries was founded on the belief that every person — regardless of background, past, or circumstance — deserves to experience the transforming love of God. What began as a small gathering in a living room has grown into a vibrant community of faith that serves hundreds of families each week.

Over the years, God has expanded our reach beyond Sunday mornings. Today, Restoration Ministries operates community outreach programs, counseling services, youth initiatives, and a growing online presence — all with the same heart that started it all: to see lives restored.

We are not a perfect church, but we are a faithful one — committed to the journey together, in love and in truth.

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture spiritual growth, and serve our community with compassion — bringing restoration, hope, and healing to all who come through our doors.

🌅

Our Vision

To be a thriving, diverse community of transformed people who reflect the love of Christ in every neighborhood, workplace, and corner of our city — and beyond.
